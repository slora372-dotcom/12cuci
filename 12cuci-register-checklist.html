12Cuci Register: What You Need Before You Sign Up

A 12Cuci register takes about a minute, but it goes faster if you have everything ready first. Here is a short checklist, plus the mistakes that most often stop new players from finishing their sign-up.

12Cuci register checklist

  • A Malaysian mobile number you own. It becomes your login ID, so use one you will keep.
  • A new password. You type it twice, so pick something you will remember.
  • Your date of birth. You must be 18 or older to register.
  • How you heard about 12Cuci. The form asks you to choose SMS, WhatsApp, Recommend or Others.
  • A referral code. If you register through a friend's link, the code is filled in for you.

The full field-by-field steps are in the 12Cuci register guide.

Common 12Cuci register mistakes

  • Typing the number with spaces or +60. Enter it like 0123456789.
  • Passwords that do not match. Tap the eye icon to check both boxes before submitting.
  • Registering twice. One account per person. If you already have an account, use 12Cuci login instead.
  • Clearing the referral code. Leave it as it is so your referrer gets credit.

After your 12Cuci register

  1. Make your first deposit from RM1. See the deposit guide.
  2. Every RM10 deposit also earns a number in the daily lucky draw.
  3. Share your own referral link to earn the RM10 free share and commission.

Ready? Tap Register to open the form.
